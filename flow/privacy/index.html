Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23

This policy covers the Abalone Mac app and the abalone.ai website. It is one policy for everyone, wherever you live and wherever you signed up. It is written in plain words so you can read all of it in a few minutes.

1. Who we are

Abalone is run by Vincent Wei Pte. Ltd., a company registered in Singapore, at 167 Jalan Bukit Merah, #05-12, Connection One, Singapore 150167. We decide how your data is used (we are the "data controller").

Contact us about anything in this policy, including our data protection officer, at hello@abalone.ai.

2. The short version

  • We keep your data for as long as your account is open, with no time limit, so your history, chats and agents are there whenever you come back.
  • We use it to run Abalone and to make Abalone better. One switch, Help improve Abalone, keeps your content out of the work to make it better (section 5).
  • We never sell your data and never show you ads.
  • OpenAI, Anthropic and Cerebras, the AI companies that answer on our account, don't train their models on what we send them.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time. We then delete everything that identifies you, and keep only data that can no longer be linked to you and the billing records the law requires (section 9).

5. Help improve Abalone

We study what people do in Abalone to make it better: for example, looking at dictations and polished text to find mistakes, turning real requests into tests, tuning our prompts, and training or tuning AI models that power Abalone.

  • This is on by default. Turn it off any time with Help improve Abalone in Settings → General, under your account. If you're in the EU or UK, this switch is how you use your right to object to this use.
  • When it's off, we stop using your content to improve Abalone, including what you made before. Everything still works the same, and your history stays.
  • We never use what you've deleted.
  • Before we use content this way, we take out names, emails and other details that point to you where we can, and only a small number of people at Abalone can see it.
  • Content you made before the date at the top of this policy is never used this way. Our earlier policy said "No AI training on your data", and we keep that promise for everything made under it.
  • Usage rows and crash reports, which hold none of your words, are always used to fix bugs and plan the product.

6. What leaves your Mac

  • Dictation runs on your Mac by default. Audio goes to ElevenLabs only in Cloud mode, or for a short time after install while the on-device model downloads, with your dictionary words so names come out right. Choose On Device in Settings → Dictation & Scribe and dictating sends nothing to any AI company: the audio and the words stay on your Mac. (When you're signed in, your history and audio files still sync to your Abalone account, and Scribe, which always uses cloud AI, includes a few of your recent dictations.)
  • Polish (Auto and Cloud modes) sends the words you said, the app name, window title and your dictionary words to Cerebras, or to OpenAI when Cerebras is down. In On Device mode the words are pasted as spoken and nothing is sent.
  • Auto-learn (Auto and Cloud modes, while you're signed in): when you fix a word right after dictating, what speech-to-text heard, the polished text, the text in the box after your change, your dictionary words and your name go to OpenAI, to find the word to add to your dictionary. In On Device mode it learns from your fix on your Mac and sends nothing. Turn it off in Settings → Dictation & Scribe.
  • Scribe always uses cloud AI. It sends your spoken request, the app name, window title, the text in the box, your first name and a few of your recent dictations to OpenAI (or Anthropic as a backup). It reads the window you're in only if you said yes once; turn that off with Screen context in Settings → Dictation & Scribe.
  • Audio files are saved to your account when you're signed in, so you can retry a transcription later.
  • Agents run in our cloud. Your messages, files, memory pages, the folder paths you point an agent at, and what its tools read go to the AI that answers. The same AI names each new chat from its first message and the start of its reply. When you make a new agent, it also reads the names of your other agents, the titles of your newest chats and the apps you dictate into most, to suggest jobs it could do.

8. How long we keep it

  • While your account is open, we keep your data, with no time limit.
  • When you delete a dictation, chat or agent, it disappears from all your devices at once. A deleted chat's files and pictures leave our storage within a minute. We keep the rest until you delete your account; email us if you want something erased sooner.
  • A few working records are kept only for a short time because they have no further use: an agent's run state (until the run ends), each step on your Mac (about 10 minutes and at most an hour; a file change you approved stays until it's done), ChatGPT sign-in attempts (a day), the note of which chat waits for an app sign-in (about an hour), and earlier versions of memory pages (when that page next changes, versions more than 30 days old are removed, apart from the one in use 30 days ago).

9. Deleting your account

Delete your account with Delete account in Settings → General, under your account, or email us.

  • Your subscription ends at once and your card is removed from Stripe within minutes.
  • We delete everything that identifies you: your profile, email and name, dictations, audio, dictionary, chats and their files, agents, memory pages, schedules, skills and devices; we ask Composio to delete your app logins and E2B to delete your cloud computer. This is done within 30 days, copies in our backups included.
  • What's on your own Mac stays there until you remove it. Companies in section 7 keep their own short records on their own schedules (Composio, up to a year).
  • What we keep:
    • Billing records the law requires: Stripe keeps invoices and payments for tax.
    • Data that can no longer be linked to you: usage rows with your account removed (cost, feature, model, time), totals and counts, and examples made from your content under section 5, only once nothing in them names or points to anyone.
    • A short record that your account was deleted and when, to show we did it.
    • Anything a court order or an abuse case requires, only for as long as it requires.
  • We will never try to link this data back to you, and anyone we share it with must promise the same.

10. Your rights

Everyone, wherever you live, can:

  • get a copy of your data
  • correct it
  • delete it, or your whole account
  • turn off product improvement (section 5)
  • complain to your data protection regulator (in Singapore, the Personal Data Protection Commission)

If you're in the EU or UK, you can also object to any use based on our legitimate interest, and take your data to another service in a machine-readable form.

If you're in California, you can also ask what we collect and why. We don't sell or share your data for ads, and we won't treat you differently for using any of these rights.

Email us at the address in section 1. We reply within 30 days.

11. What we never do

  • Show ads or share your data with ad networks.
  • Track you across other apps or websites.
