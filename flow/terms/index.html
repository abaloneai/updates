Usage limits

Every plan includes a set amount of AI use, shared by your agents, Scribe, polish and cloud dictation. It's counted two ways:

  • A weekly limit. When the week's use is used up, the features that use it stop until it resets. Dictation on your Mac keeps working.
  • A five-hour limit. Agents also stop when a five-hour session is used up, until it resets. Dictation and Scribe keep working until the week is used up.

Settings → Usage & Billing shows how much of each limit you've used and when it resets (for example, “Resets in 3 hours”). Unused use doesn't carry over to the next week or session. We may change how much each plan includes; the app always shows your current limits.
