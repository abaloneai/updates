Tide Ear field notes

These notes record a small survey of abalone shells washed up along one stretch of coast over a winter.

Reference number: ABL-4471-ZEPHYR. Quote it if you write to us about these notes.

We counted 212 shells in all, most of them under 9 cm across.

With thanks to Ines Halvorsen, who drew every shell in the survey.
